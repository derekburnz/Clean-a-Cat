My pick: keep our own icons and Model-Agent renders as the main set, and use Phosphor two tone, recoloured, only for icons we have not drawn yet. Phosphor filled reads well except the coins, which blur at 48 px, Lucide is too thin over the world and Kenney, even with our outline and lip added, keeps its pale flat shading and small, pixel-edged corners, so it reads more generic than ours. The Kenney files here come from a GitHub mirror of the older 148 file pack, because kenney.nl is blocked from this machine.
