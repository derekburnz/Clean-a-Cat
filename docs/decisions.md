## Decided
2026-11-23: Art style is Bubble Pastel colours (cream #FFF6EC, mint #A8E6CF, bubblegum #FFB3C7, sky #8FD3FE, mud #5A4632) on the blocky chibi shapes of Derek's asset library, written up in `docs/art/style-guide.md`. 2026-11-25: The design is agreed: Clean a Cat is a busy cat spa with a cat collection. Waiting cats leave when their heart bar is empty, paid skips sit on every build, the starter pack runs a real 72 hour countdown, six players share a server and other players can walk into a spa unless its owner turns that off. The one page design is `docs/design/clean-a-cat/gdd.md`. Why: it is the owner's playstyle pick after nine playtests and his answers to all 65 plan questions, joined to the one thing lasting games share, which is something to collect. (Derek, written up by Planning-Agent)

2026-11-26: Each hand step of the wash takes about 4 to 5 seconds, behind the switch washPace (issue 865): brushing 8 strokes at least 0.45 s apart (was 3 at 0.2, about 1 s), the lather 26 studs of rubbing (was 12), the rinse a narrower spray, the claw hold 0.8 s a paw and the scrub 2 studs of sponge a mud patch (was 3.6, about 8 s to about 4.5). Why: Derek after a playtest, "the brushing fur minigame goes way too fast they should take around 4-5 seconds each minigame". With the scrub shorter, a level 1 cat's scrub and brush go from about 9 s to about 9 s (scrub 8 to 4.5, brush 1 to 4.5), so the first session's pace in minutes stays about the same; level 2 and up cats with a bath or claws take about 5 s longer. (Design-Agent)
