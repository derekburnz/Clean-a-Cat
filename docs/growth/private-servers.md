# Private servers: what Roblox's setting allows (Growth-Agent, updated 2026-11-26)

Sources: Roblox Creator Hub pages "Private servers" and "Roblox Plus" (read 2026-11-26). OFFICIAL = on those pages. JUDGMENT = ours. No prices here: this repo is public; the chosen price is in the library.

- OFFICIAL: Creator Dashboard, Audience, Access Settings: "Enable Requires Robux" and an amount, or off to make private servers free for everyone. One price per game, not per player, and it is a monthly fee.
- OFFICIAL: no minimum or maximum price is stated, so a low price is not ruled out, but not confirmed either; the Access Settings field is the proof.
- OFFICIAL: Roblox Plus subscribers get access to paid private servers at no cost, whatever the price.
- OFFICIAL: changing the price cancels every active private server subscription, with a message to each player. Set it once.
- OFFICIAL: the Plus payout to the game is 70 percent of the server price, up to 100 Robux per Plus subscriber who spends 60 minutes in their own paid private server in 30 days. A lower price pays less.
- OFFICIAL: players under 13 may not be able to join private servers, depending on their privacy and parental control settings. The game never tells them they are missing anything.
- JUDGMENT: a low price has little downside for players (no real benefit to being alone in your own server); the cost is a smaller Plus payout. Set on promotion day, once. The game must be public first.
- Group walls: Roblox retired them in March 2026, so there is nothing to switch off.
